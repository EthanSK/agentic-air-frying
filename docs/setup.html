1 / Fryer

COSORI Turbo Tower Pro Smart

The 10.8 L Pro Smart has a 4.3 L top drawer and a 6.5 L bottom drawer. The bottom has upper and lower heaters. The Wi-Fi model is CAF-DC113S. The suffix is AUK for the UK model and AEU for the EU model.

  1. Check for Pro Smart and your region’s model number before buying. The other Turbo Tower variants have different features.
  2. Set it on a level, heat-resistant surface with the vents clear. Follow the space requirements in the manual for your unit.
  3. Wash and dry the removable parts and run the supervised first-use test from your manual before cooking food.

2 / App

Pair VeSync

  1. Install VeSync and sign in. Put your phone on your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and switch the fryer on at its panel.
  2. Tap Add (+) in VeSync, select the Pro Smart and follow the pairing prompts. Open the fryer tile and check that both zones appear.
  3. Use VeSync on your phone or open it through iPhone Mirroring. On a compatible Apple silicon Mac, you can also use the iPad app if the Mac App Store offers it.

3 / Order History

Find Recent Orders

Use a nearby iPhone with iOS 18 or later and a Mac with Apple silicon or a T2 chip running macOS Sequoia 15 or later. Sign both devices into the same Apple Account with two-factor authentication, enable Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and lock the iPhone before connecting. Availability varies by region.

  1. Enable Codex Computer Use. In the Codex desktop app on the Mac, open Settings → Computer use. Install or enable Any App under Control and follow the macOS permission prompts.
  2. Install Zapp or Zoom by Ocado if it delivers to you. Sign in to your own account on the iPhone.
  3. Open iPhone Mirroring on the Mac and complete Apple’s setup prompts. Confirm you can open the grocery app from the mirrored phone.
  4. Ask Codex to use Computer Use to check recent delivered orders before planning dinner. Use the order history in Zapp or Orders in Zoom to find product names, pack sizes and quantities.
  5. Check what’s still available and fresh or frozen when the order alone doesn’t establish it. Receipts, package directions or a short list also work.

4 / First Cook

Set Both Drawers

  1. Ask for a plan with the food and settings for each drawer. Check the cooking directions and load the food.
  2. Set both zones, or ask your assistant to send your confirmed program once through VeSync. Confirm the mode, temperature, time and Sync or Match setting.
  3. When VeSync shows Press the Start Button, press Start/Pause on the fryer. Check that the expected zone shows Cooking and its time counts down. With Sync, the shorter zone can wait on Hold.
  4. Stay nearby, follow the appliance manual and check the food before serving.

Optional / Hardware Experiment

Foil-Tipped Fingerbot

Ethan mounted a MOES BS-FB-V3 Bluetooth Fingerbot with white duct tape across the body and used Blu Tack to hold an aluminium foil contact on the moving arm. An exposed foil tip registered a touch in a manual trial. The mounted bot’s automatic Start remains unverified.

  1. Pair the Bluetooth Fingerbot in the MOES, Smart Life or Tuya app. Off the appliance, use Click mode and verify the arm presses once and retracts.
  2. For a fit check, unplug the cool fryer. Put a small, smooth piece of kitchen foil on the arm tip with Blu Tack behind it. Leave the metal face exposed towards the touch panel.
  3. Hold the bot in position and align the foil over the centre of Start/Pause. Check that it clears the panel at rest, reaches it at the end of travel and cannot brush other controls. Keep the vent, drawer and manual access clear.
  4. Use manual Start for cooking. COSORI specifies physical Start for this model and excludes separate remote control of the appliance.
